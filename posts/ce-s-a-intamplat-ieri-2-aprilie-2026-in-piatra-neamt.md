In Piatra Neamț, a fire broke out yesterday afternoon in a residential building located on Strada Ștefan cel Mare. The local firefighters intervened promptly, managing to contain the blaze without casualties, but several apartments suffered damage. Authorities have started an investigation to determine the exact cause of the incident, pointing to a possible electrical malfunction.

##  Accidents

A traffic accident occurred in the early morning hours on DN15 near the city limits of Piatra Neamț. A collision between two cars led to minor injuries for three people, who were transported to the County Emergency Hospital. Police are investigating the circumstances, with initial reports suggesting that slippery road conditions and reduced visibility might have contributed to the accident.

Such initiatives are part of the school’s ongoing commitment to sustainability education.

##  Urban Development

The City Hall of Piatra Neamț approved the final stage of the modernization project for Strada Cuza Vodă, which will improve pedestrian access and upgrade public lighting. The works are slated to begin in the next quarter and are funded through both local budgets and European Union grants. Residents have welcomed the upgrade, anticipating greater safety and accessibility in this busy area.

##  Environment

A new urban forestry program was launched in Piatra Neamț aiming to plant 1,000 trees across various neighborhoods by the end of the year. The initiative, part of the city’s environmental strategy, seeks to improve air quality and enhance urban biodiversity. Volunteers will collaborate with municipal services to ensure proper care and maintenance of the newly planted trees.

During yesterday’s city council meeting, the mayor discussed upcoming strategies to improve public transportation services, with a focus on increasing the frequency of bus routes and integrating eco-friendly vehicles. Council members also debated budget allocations for infrastructure improvements, highlighting the need for efficient spending to support sustainable urban growth.
